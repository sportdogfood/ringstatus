## One set of meanings

1. Identity, dated scope, membership/coverage and evidence integrity are validated before use. A recent timestamp cannot cure a conflict or incomplete coverage.
2. Original source-observation time establishes age. Replay, archive publication, sync, verification and handoff do not renew it. A genuinely new scheduled source fetch has a new observation time even if content is unchanged; unchanged content does not establish new progress or a finish time. Collection completion is process completion, not competition finish time.
3. Preserve evidence quality separately from lifecycle: fresh/stale/absent/identity-conflict are not interchangeable with active/completed. Use existing status, payload and reason fields; do not invent one new overloaded status column.
4. Absent means no qualifying evidence exists. Stale means retained evidence exists but is too old for the requested current action. Identity conflict means evidence cannot be attributed safely; withhold the affected decision and retain the reason.
5. Verified provider-reported group completion and its separate phase counts remain historical evidence after active freshness expires. They do not renew active pace, certify class/trip completion, prove every scheduled group was covered, or establish actual finish time. Counts and completion status must not be silently made equivalent. Preserve contradictory counts alongside the reported status, withholding unsupported calculations; an identity conflict separately blocks attribution. Do not erase the observation merely because its status and counts disagree.
6. Final means the exact level and facts supported by the saved response, not permanent immunity to later correction. A newer verified correction/reopening must supersede it under the existing identity/evidence rules. Historical evidence is displayed with its original time and provenance.
7. Per-record missing/stale/conflicting evidence is logged and withheld without inventing a match or blocking unrelated work. Unsafe writes still require durable intent and existing recovery protections.
8. For agreed overlaps, consumer effective values select applicable verified Live first while active, and Data as fallback. Preserve source identity, observation time and fallback/rejection reason using existing evidence/status/payload fields. A Data refresh must not override an applicable Live value merely because Data was written later. Missing fields must not clear existing verified observations or imply zero. Non-overlapping Data facts retain their existing authority.

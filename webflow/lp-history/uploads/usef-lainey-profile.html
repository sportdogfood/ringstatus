IMPORTANT NOTICE CONCERNING POINTS

According to GR1111.6, an owner's residence at the time he/she applies for membership will be maintained throughout the year and is considered his/her home region or zone. If the recorded owner moves his/her residence during the year to a new region or zone, points can be accumulated in the new region or zone provided the owner declares in writing to USEF that the new region or zone will be his/her home region or zone.

If the recorded owner changes states within the same home region or zone, a zone declaration should still be submitted since different states within the region or zone may have different contiguous states.

For points to count, the declaration must be received by the USEF office on or before the first recognized day of the competition.

A zone declaration must be submitted in writing via email to memberservices@usef.org. It may also be submitted via mail or fax, Attention: Customer Care-Zone Declaration.

If you have any questions please contact Member Services at (859) 810 8733. Member Services is available M-F 8:30 am to 5 pm ET.

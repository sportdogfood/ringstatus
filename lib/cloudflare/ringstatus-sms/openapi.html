About

Built for the people who keep the day moving.

RingStatus helps riders, trainers, grooms, and show teams see what matters fast — without digging through heavy schedules or waiting on scattered updates.

Founder

I build tools from the ground level of the horse show.

This started with a simple problem: riders and barns need better visibility during long, changing show days. RingStatus is designed around the way people actually move through a showground.

The goal is simple.

Less guessing. Less chasing information. More confidence getting horses and riders to the right place at the right time.

Company

RingStatus is show-day intelligence for busy barns.

We focus on lightweight tools that make class timing, ring status, watchlists, and barn communication easier to manage from a phone.

Fast

Designed for quick checks, not deep searching.

Mobile-first

Built for rings, barns, golf carts, and poor showground connectivity.

Rider-focused

Centered around what each rider, trainer, groom, or barn needs to know.

Mission

Make the show day easier to read.

RingStatus turns live schedule data into a cleaner, role-focused view so barns can act faster when the day changes.
